# 📊 Trading Rewards

On November 23, 2023, the dYdX community vote to migrate  the remaining amount of Trading Rewards (unvested unvested $ethDYDX in dYdX v3 [Rewards Treasury Vester](https://etherscan.io/address/0xb9431e19b29b952d9358025f680077c3fd37292f)) to dYdX Chain. DYDX now sits in the[dYdX Chain Rewards Treasury Vester `(dydx1wxje320an3karyc6mjw4zghs300dmrjkwn7xtk)`](https://www.mintscan.io/dydx/address/dydx1wxje320an3karyc6mjw4zghs300dmrjkwn7xtk)and is distributed as trading rewards on the dYdX Chain.

More information about the dYdX Chain Trading Rewards is available here.&#x20;
